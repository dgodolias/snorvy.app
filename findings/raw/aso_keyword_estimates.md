# v9 ASO Keyword Volume Estimates (US/GB/DE)

Date: 2026-05-07. $0-cost research. Tools: WebSearch, public articles, Sensor Tower public preview snippets, Play Store result-page density (proxy for competitive density).

**Methodology gap**: AppTweak / Sensor Tower / data.ai keyword-volume APIs are paywalled. SerpApi Play Store keyword volume = paid. Google Keyword Planner reports *web* search not Play Store search. So volumes below are **proxy-derived ranges** (competitor count + their install bands + public BOA case studies). Scale: VL/L/M/H/VH (~ <2k / 2-10k / 10-30k / 30-100k / 100k+ monthly searches Play US).

| invoice app uk small business | **L** (2-5k/mo GB) | Low | UK-localised, weak coverage. Xero + QB own the SEO; Play Store apps rarely localise UK copy. |

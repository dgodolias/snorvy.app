## Per-keyword commentary

### "couples app" / app para casais / çift uygulaması
- **BR (High)**: Couples apps are well-established (Couplete, Lasting, Between clones). PT-BR Play Store has dozens of "app para casais" listings. Search demand is consistent.
- **MX (Med)**: "App para parejas" is searched but Mexican consumers lean to free social apps (WhatsApp, Telegram) over dedicated couples tools. Lower per-capita compared to BR.
- **TR (Med)**: "Çift uygulaması" / "sevgili uygulaması" pulls multiple Turkish couples apps (Couple, Lovify with Turkish UI). Turkish dating culture is mobile-first.
- **EG / SA (Low)**: Couples/relationship apps face cultural friction in MENA — public-facing relationship tracking is uncommon. Search volume reflects this. Privacy-first or marriage-framed angle could grow demand.

### "mapa astral" / carta astral / doğum haritası
- **BR (Very High)**: Brazil ranks **#4 globally** in astral-map search interest per public Google Trends data. 42% of Brazilians believe in astrology influence. "Mapa astral" is one of the highest-volume astrology terms in any language. Astrolink's existence (PT-native) confirms commercial validation.
- **MX (High)**: "Carta astral" is the standard ES term. Strong demand, less saturated than BR.
- **TR (Med–High)**: "Doğum haritası" + "burç" (zodiac) demand is real — Faladdin (Turkish-origin tarot/horoscope app) has 25M users globally, mostly TR-anchored. Astrology/fortune-telling is mainstream in Turkish culture.
- **EG / SA (Low–Med)**: Astrology in Arabic is **religiously restricted** (haram per most scholars). Demand exists but underground; Play Store keyword volumes will look low even if private interest is higher. Risky niche.

### "tarot"
- **TR (Very High)**: Turkey is the **single largest tarot/horoscope app market per capita** — Faladdin alone hits 25M users. "Tarot" + "kahve falı" (coffee fortune) is core Turkish demand. Highest volume of any keyword × country cell here.
- **BR / MX (High)**: Tarot is mainstream in Latin America. PT/ES tarot apps proliferate.
- **EG / SA (Med)**: "تاروت" has decent search volume despite religious tension; lots of Arabic users in Egypt and KSA browse tarot content via web/TikTok. Underserved on Play Store with native-Arabic apps.

### "dormir bien" / dormir melhor / iyi uyumak
- **All countries Med to Low–Med**: Sleep-tracking is a global mid-tier category. BR/MX have growing wellness markets but sleep specifically lags meditation/calm apps. TR has demand but Sleep Cycle already fully localizes (see localization_audit.md). MENA sleep-app demand is constrained — wellness vocab still translates poorly culturally; "نوم جيد" returns mostly hadith/religious content, not app intent.

### "manifestation" / manifestação / manifestación / manifest / تجلي
- **BR (Med–High, growing)**: Manifestation/Lei da Atração is a rising Brazilian niche — TikTok-driven, strong creator economy push (Nikki Maluly, Pri Leite). Play Store volume is mid but trajectory is clearly up.
- **MX (Med)**: "Manifestación" + "ley de atracción" — established but smaller than BR.
- **TR / EG / SA (Low)**: "Manifest" in Turkish is more associated with public-protest meaning than law-of-attraction. Arabic "تجلي" carries Sufi/religious connotation, not new-age. Concept doesn't translate into a clear Play Store keyword in these markets — different cultural angle (intentions, dua, niyyah) needed.

## Confidence + caveats

- **High confidence** ranks: BR mapa astral (Very High), TR tarot (Very High), BR couples app (High), Faladdin-validated TR fortune-telling demand.
- **Medium confidence** ranks: ES carta astral, MX manifestación, BR manifestação trajectory.
- **Lower confidence** ranks: All EG/SA cells — Arabic Play Store keyword data is the least published publicly; numbers are extrapolated from web Google Trends + cultural reasoning.
- These are **search volumes**, not download volumes. A high-search keyword in a market saturated with localized incumbents (e.g., TR tarot vs. Faladdin) is harder to win than a Med-search keyword with no localized competitor (e.g., AR dating with dialect support).

## Implications for direction-ranking

1. **TR tarot** = highest-volume × machine-translated Labyrinthos competitor = top-tier opportunity (already in directions.md community-1).
2. **BR mapa astral** = Very High volume but Astrolink is PT-native — beatable only on UX/social/AI angle, not language gap.
3. **BR manifestação** = growing trajectory, defensible if entered now.
4. **MENA tarot/astrology** = real underground demand, religious-tension risk, requires culturally-aware framing (e.g., "personality types" instead of explicit astrology).
5. **MX/BR couples apps** = high search but very saturated — differentiation must be feature-based (e.g., long-distance, anti-cheating, shared finances).

## Key takeaways for Maktub model

1. **The only fully verifiable subscription comparable is Sleep Cycle: $24.7M ARR / 918K paying subs = ~$27 ARPU/yr (~$2.25/MAU/mo).** This is a *non-couples utility*, but it sets a realistic ceiling — premium global app with public financials, decade-old brand, generates only $27/user/yr.
2. **Love8 — the most directly comparable couples app — has not disclosed a number for a reason.** Apptopia/AppMagic listings exist but Love8's owner (Chongqing Daole Er Tech) has never published, and third-party estimators put MRR in low six figures. **Couples apps are not a known scaled-revenue category.**
3. **buzzArab is ad-supported only**, after ~10 years in the MENA dating market with 420K members. This is a strong negative signal for paid-sub willingness in MENA dating/relationship apps.
4. **Astrolink is private and not financially transparent.** Any "Astrolink revenue" claim in our pitch must be explicitly flagged as estimate.
5. **The $30–60/MAU/mo assumption in Direction 2 is unsupported by any disclosed comparable in the dataset.** Sleep Cycle's $2.25/MAU/mo is the only verified number, and that's a global utility with strong WHO-funded sleep-health positioning.

**Recommendation:** revise market models to use Sleep Cycle's $2–3/MAU/mo as anchor, with a 3–5x multiplier ceiling for tight-niche couples apps ($6–15/MAU/mo). Anything above that requires a real disclosed comparable we don't currently have.

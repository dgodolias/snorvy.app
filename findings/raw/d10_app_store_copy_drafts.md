> **"Bosan dengan aplikasi e-invois yang lambat dan rumit? e-Invois Cepat menjadikan penghantaran invois LHDN-patuh semudah hantar WhatsApp — dalam Bahasa Melayu, terus dari telefon anda."**

English: "Tired of slow, complicated e-invoice apps? e-Invois Cepat makes LHDN-compliant invoice submission as easy as sending a WhatsApp — in Bahasa Melayu, straight from your phone."

Legal-safe: doesn't name LHDN or MyInvois, attacks "slow and complicated" as generic category problem. WhatsApp comparison is fair-use simile.

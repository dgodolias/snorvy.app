# D10 — Phase 4 Mandate Timeline & Launch Urgency

**Date:** 2026-05-07 (Tick 20)
**Source:** ClearTax MY, Jomeinvoice, LHDN FAQ PDF, BiographyPoints news, Banqup, Biztrak

## Exact Phase 4 trigger date

**1 January 2026** — businesses with annual turnover **RM1 million – RM5 million** must comply.
**Relaxation period:** until **31 December 2026** (consolidated invoices allowed without prosecution).
**Full enforcement:** **1 January 2027** — penalties begin in earnest.

Additional Jan 2026 trigger: **All transactions above RM10,000 require individual e-invoice** (no more consolidated). Affects ALL businesses regardless of phase.

## Penalty structure for non-compliance

- **RM200 – RM20,000 per non-compliant invoice** under Section 82C, Income Tax Act 1967
- **Up to RM20,000 fine OR 6 months imprisonment** under tax law
- Applies per invoice — a freelancer with 50 missed invoices could face theoretical RM1M exposure
- Section 120 prosecution paused only during 6-month grace per phase

## Launch window analysis

### Why launch NOW (May 2026)

1. **Phase 4 already live** — 150K MSMEs in grace period, actively seeking better tools NOW (not Jan)
2. **Phase 5 is 8 weeks away** (1 Jul 2026) — 600K micro-businesses entering anxiety phase
3. **Grace ends 31 Dec 2026** — peak panic from Oct-Dec 2026 = peak conversion window
4. **Competitor saturation NOT YET HIT** — Bukku, InvoisPro, JomeInvoice exist but none mobile-native + Bahasa-first

| Period | Phase | D10 Action |

## Anti-positioning hook (legal-safe)

> *"Tired of time-tracking apps that lock your hours behind a subscription paywall? HourLog is a one-time purchase. No monthly fee, no team minimums, no surprise upgrades to read last month's timesheet. Track billable hours, export CSV/PDF, sync across two devices — and own it forever."*

**Legal safety:**
- Never names Clockify/Toggl/QB Workforce specifically
- "Subscription paywall" is a generic industry term — protected
- "Bait-and-switch" is NOT used in copy (claim risk in some jurisdictions)
- Does not assert competitor product defects, only category-wide friction

## Anti-features (do NOT include)

- No team management / no multi-user dashboard (out of scope for solo dev)
- No payroll / paystub features (don't compete with QB Workforce on its strong axis)
- No always-on background tracking (RescueTime turf, privacy risk)
- No in-app subscriptions (kills the wedge)
- No login/account required for v1 (auth = top-3 Clockify complaint per verbatim #1)

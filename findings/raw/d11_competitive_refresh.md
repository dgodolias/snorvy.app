# D11 HourLog — Competitive Refresh (Tick 34)

Date: 2026-05-07
Direction: D11 HourLog (US time tracker, solo-trades focus)

## Incumbent Moves

### Clockify
- **Free tier nerf (April 2026):** Free plan capped at 5 users — previously unlimited. Major shift; squeezes growing solo crews.
- 2026 added **Location Alerts** to mobile (lightweight; not full geofencing — Timeero/Buddy Punch still lead).
- Mobile admin gaps remain: cannot create schedules, manage kiosks, approve timesheets, add clients, send invoices, set rates from mobile.
- Last app update active in 2026 (no major redesign found).

### Toggl Track
- **Time-off feature** released Dec 2025 (in testing).
- Mobile remains a "lite" companion — fewer options than browser; users still report sync failure indicators.
- No price change found.
- No major mobile redesign in 2026.

### QuickBooks Workforce
- **April 2026 expansion:** Now positioned as integrated payroll + time + HR + benefits suite (employer side).
- New employee fields: Preferred Name, Department, Manager, Emergency Contact.
- Document upload/share added in Workforce portal.
- Hourly/non-hourly cost split into multiple classes + projects (deeper Intuit ecosystem lock-in).
- Last update April 9, 2026.
- **Effect on D11:** Workforce moves further into employer/HR territory — drifts AWAY from solo-trades self-employed wedge. Validates D11 gap.

## D11 Window Status

- **Clockify free-tier nerf** is a TAILWIND — micro-crews (1-5 trades) hit cap, looking for cheap mobile-first alternative.
- **Toggl** still desktop-first; mobile parity unsolved.
- **Workforce** moving up-market (HR suite); leaves solo-trades unattended.
- **No new entrants** found targeting US solo-trades hour logger.

## Verdict

Window OPEN; slight tailwind from Clockify free-tier cap (April 2026). No urgency change needed but D11 wedge marginally stronger.

# D13 Bloki — 30-Day Post-Launch Revenue Model + Y1 ARR

**Launch:** Jul 6, 2026 | **Mandate trigger:** Oct 1, 2026
**TAM:** ~700k Greek freelancers + small biz mandated to use myDATA
**Competing apps Greek-only:** ~5 (most ERP-tier, none mobile-native)

## Assumptions

- ASA spend: €350 over 30 days = €11.67/day
- ASA CPI Greece for tax/finance keywords: ~€0.40-0.80 (small market, low competition)
- Organic via TikTok + r/greece + insomnia.gr posts (3 channels, free)
- Paid tier: €4.99/mo (introductory), €7.99 from Oct 1
- Free tier limit: 10 invoices/month → soft paywall

## Y1 ARR Projection (Jul 2026 → Jun 2027)

The mandate on Oct 1 is the inflection. Modeled as 3 phases.

| Phase | Months | Driver |
|---|---|---|
| Soft (Jul-Sep) | 3 | ASA + organic, no urgency |
| Mandate spike (Oct-Dec) | 3 | Compliance panic, virality |
| Steady-state (Jan-Jun) | 6 | Retention + word-of-mouth |

## Break-Even Timeline

**Costs:**
- ASA: €350 (one-time)
- Domain + trademark: €110
- Accountant reviews: €230
- Play + Apple dev fees: €25 + €99 = €124
- **Total Y1 hard cost: ~€814**

**Break-even:** Hit at ~165 paid subs at €4.99 → reached **mid-September 2026** (base scenario), well before mandate.

## Key Sensitivities

1. **Mandate enforcement strictness** — if AADE delays Oct 1 (it has before), the spike flattens by 30-50%
2. **Existing competitor mobile launch** — none yet, but a SoftOne or Elorus mobile app would compress paid conversion to 2-3%
3. **TikTok virality** — 1 viral creator video could double organic, this is the asymmetric upside
4. **Provider-track regret** — if Bloki later wants to be a full e-invoicing PROVIDER (not just ERP user), ~6 months of regulated certification (separate decision)

## Verdict

**Realistic Y1 ARR: €95k-€160k base/pessimistic floor.**
Break-even by September. Mandate is a hard catalyst, not a hope.

## Notes for the developer

- Avoid naming "Invoice Simple" anywhere in the App Store / Play Store text — Apple and Google reject "comparison apps that mention competitors by name." The "Tired of subscription apps tripling their price?" pattern stays inside the legal safe zone (anonymous category critique).
- For the landing page (off-store) you CAN name Invoice Simple in comparison tables — that's covered under nominative fair use in US/UK/EU.
- Keep the privacy policy LITERALLY one page. The "1-page privacy policy" claim in the description is a differentiation hook against Invoice Simple's 4-page policy.

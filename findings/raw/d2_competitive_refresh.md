# D2 SoloLedger — Competitive Refresh (Tick 34)

Date: 2026-05-07
Direction: D2 SoloLedger (US Schedule C estimator, solo/freelance, $0-budget mobile-first)

## Intuit Moves — QuickBooks Solopreneur

- **QBSE → Solopreneur migration ongoing.** Self-Employed legacy product being sunset; existing subscribers can keep or upgrade.
- **Pricing:** $20/mo or $120/yr (with 30-day free trial / 50% off first 3 months).
- **July 2025 price hike: 15-20% across all Intuit plans.** Trajectory: Simple Start $25 (2020) → $30 (2022) → $35 (2024) → $38 (2026).
- Intuit attributes hikes to AI development (Intuit Assist), automation, and infrastructure.
- Solopreneur features: easier setup vs QBSE, improved transaction mgmt, growth tools, **Schedule C filing seamlessly into TurboTax**.
- Payments updated: card, PayPal, Venmo, Apple Pay, ACH.
- Forecast: 12-15% further annual hikes expected.

## Effect on D2 Thesis

- **STRONG TAILWIND:** Solopreneur at $20/mo = $240/yr is now expensive for low-revenue freelancers/giggers.
- Price-out gap widens for the long-tail: Uber/DoorDash drivers, Etsy sellers, OnlyFans creators, Twitch streamers — anyone netting <$30K who balks at $240/yr.
- D2 wedge ($0-cost mobile estimator with Schedule C focus) becomes more attractive vs QBSE/Solopreneur cost.
- TurboTax tie-in is Intuit's lock-in — D2 doesn't compete on filing, only **estimation + quarterly planning**, which Intuit upcharges.

## New Entrants

- No major new "Schedule C estimator app" launch in 2026 found.
- Existing competitors: Keeper Tax (quarterly calc), Tax My Gig, SDOCPA — mostly web calculators, not native mobile apps.
- TurboTax TaxCaster, H&R Block calculator remain web-first.
- **Mobile-native Schedule C estimator gap unfilled.**

## D2 Window Status

- Window OPEN and WIDENING due to Intuit price escalation.
- Mobile-native, ad-free, free-to-use Schedule C estimator wedge looks stronger than at v9 ranking.
- Could justify slight ranking uplift relative to D11.

## Verdict

D2 thesis STRENGTHENED. Recent Intuit moves push more freelancers into the "I just need a quick estimator" segment. No urgent new entrant; window remains open.

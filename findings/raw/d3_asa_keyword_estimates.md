## Top 5 by Composite

1. **stride alternative** — 10.00 (low vol but pure intent + zero comp; capture displacement traffic)
2. **gig worker mileage** — 7.50 (mid vol, low comp; clean B2B-ish phrasing)
3. **rideshare tax tracker** — 7.50 (mid vol, low comp; tax+job combo gap)
4. **doordash mileage tracker** — 6.67 (brand+function, no incumbent owns title)
5. **uber driver tax app** — 6.67 (same brand-leverage gap as #4)

## Strategic Read
- **Brand-leverage gap**: No incumbent app TITLE contains "doordash", "uber", or "lyft" (per ASA convention all use generic "mileage tracker"). Title+subtitle play around brand+function = open lane.
- **"Stride alternative"** is the displacement keyword — Stride 3.1 stars, 20.7K ratings, 69.4% recent Android low_pct. People ARE typing "stride alternative".
- **"Gig worker"** > "self-employed" for D3: incumbents use the broader term; we own the niche term.
- **Skip**: delivery driver app, instacart shopper app, lyft driver tools (wrong intent or saturated).

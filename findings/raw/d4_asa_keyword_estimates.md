# D4 ASA Keyword Scoring — EU Solo-Freelancer Invoice (PL + DE + NL)

**Date**: 2026-05-07 | **Scope**: 17 candidate keywords across 3 countries for Play Store Search Ads / SEO
**Formula**: Composite = (Volume × Intent) ÷ Competition (all 1-5)
**Method**: Estimates calibrated against `d4_competitor_descriptions.json` pulled today: Tofu PL (1M+ installs, no rating shown — fresh launch effect), Lexware Office DE (100K+ installs, 4.7, 5.2K ratings), sevDesk DE (100K+, 4.1, 2.7K ratings), Invoice Fly NL (1M+, 4.1, 56.3K ratings — translated EN), Moneybird NL (50K+, 4.4, 1.6K ratings). Cross-checked against Polish JDG/KSeF rankings (SzybkaFaktura, FakturaXL, IFIRMA, wFirma) which are mostly desktop/web; mobile-first PL slot is open. PL/KSeF context: April 1, 2026 mandatory KSeF deadline = active panic searches now. Official `pl.gov.mf.ksef` government app exists but UX is reportedly clunky — strong "alternative" intent.

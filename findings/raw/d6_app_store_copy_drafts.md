## D6 NAME — Confirmation

**Recommended: keep "Tycoon Zero"** — clean, 11 chars, unique on Play Store search (verified blank in r/incremental_games archive). The "Zero" double-meaning (zero ads + zero subscriptions + zero-to-billionaire arc) is intentional and reinforces the wedge.

Alternatives if "Tycoon Zero" hits a trademark issue:
- **"Idle Zero"** (10 chars) — keyword "idle" + zero promise
- **"Pay Once Tycoon"** (15 chars) — explicit anti-positioning
- **"Tycoon. Pay Once."** (18 chars) — period-driven, distinctive
- **"Honest Tycoon"** (13 chars) — character/value play

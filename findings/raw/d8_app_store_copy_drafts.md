## Anti-Positioning Hook (legal-safe, attacks ShutEye trial pattern)

**Hero copy (long-form description top):**
> "Sick of free trials that charge your card on day 2? Snorvy is a one-time purchase sleep recorder. Record your snores, track your nights, own your data forever. No subscription. No trial. No surprise charges."

**Short hook (ad creative / first 80 chars):**
> "Tired of sleep apps charging $80 before the trial ends? Snorvy is $9.99 one-time."

**Legal safety notes:**
- Never name-drop "ShutEye" or competitors in copy (only in user-facing review-response language).
- Avoid claims like "we are accurate" or "more accurate than X" - unprovable, FTC risk.
- Stick to verifiable structural claims: "one-time purchase", "no subscription", "offline recording", "no account required".
- The "$80 before the trial ends" hook is a generic category complaint, not a specific accusation - safe.

**Reinforcement bullets (description body):**
- Pay $9.99 once - never again
- No account required - works fully offline
- Snore + ambient audio recorder
- Export your data as WAV/CSV anytime
- Open-source recording engine (link to GitHub)

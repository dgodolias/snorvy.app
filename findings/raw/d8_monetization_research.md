## 1. Indie pay-once apps — disclosed financials

### Things 3 (Cultured Code)
- Pricing: $9.99 iPhone / $19.99 iPad / $49.99 Mac (one-time per platform). Apple Watch bundled with iPhone version.
- Revenue/ARR: NOT VERIFIED publicly. Crunchbase third-party estimate ~$192K (likely undercount of a single-platform proxy, not authoritative).
- Team size: 12 people in Stuttgart, Germany — per official About page ([Cultured Code About](https://culturedcode.com/about/)).
- Founded: 2007 (Things 1 launched 2008). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Things_(software)))
- Major paid upgrade history: Things 1 (2008) → Things 2 (2012) → Things 3 (May 2017). 20% launch-week discount; free upgrade only if you bought Things 2 between Mar 1 – May 17 2017. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2017/05/18/things-3-task-manager-for-mac-ios-and-apple-watch/))
- **Takeaway for Snorvy**: Per-platform pay-once with paid major version every 4-5 yrs sustains a 12-person team for 17+ years — proves the model.

### Procreate (Savage Interactive)
- Pricing: $12.99 iPad one-time, no subscription ever ([Facebook/Procreate launch post](https://www.facebook.com/procreate/posts/its-here-1999-usd-no-subscriptionsprocreate-dreams-is-available-on-the-app-store/731816305648044/)).
- Revenue: **$30M+ annual revenue**, disclosed by CEO James Cuda on Grow A Small Business podcast March 2020. ([podcast page](https://growasmallbusiness.libsyn.com/0011-doing-30m-in-sales-with-40-team-members-in-2011-shut-down-a-successful-digital-agency-to-back-an-ipad-idea-and-push-through-the-hard-transition-times-when-they-were-down-to-their-last-few-dollars-in-the-bank-james-cuda)) Recent Sensor Tower estimate: ~$2M/month, ~200K downloads/month (March 2026).
- Team: 40 staff. Self-funded (founder + spouse only investors). ([Stockhead profile](https://stockhead.com.au/tech/how-a-hobart-app-developer-partnered-with-apple-to-became-a-global-powerhouse/))
- Founded: 2011 (relaunch).
- Paid upgrade: Procreate 5 (2019) was a free update. New product Procreate Dreams (2023) is a separate $19.99 one-time SKU — sidestepping the upgrade question via a sister app.
- **Takeaway**: $13 anchor price + giant volume + sister-app SKUs = scalable. Procreate proves $9.99-tier can hit eight figures.

### Streaks (Crunchy Bagel)
- Pricing: $5.99 iPhone one-time (formerly $4.99). ([streaksapp.com](https://streaksapp.com/privacy.html))
- Revenue: NOT VERIFIED.
- Team: Single developer Quentin Zervaas (Adelaide, AU). ([CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/crunchy-bagel))
- Founded: 2015. Apple Design Award 2016.
- Paid upgrade: Streaks 7 released Jan 2024 — free update for existing customers. Solo dev relies on new-customer flow, not upgrade revenue.
- **Takeaway**: Solo dev sub-$10 pay-once apps survive on slow burn + Apple editorial.

### Forest (Seekrtech)
- Pricing: $1.99 iOS one-time + cosmetic IAP coins (Android version is freemium with sub). ([forestapp.cc](https://www.forestapp.cc/))
- Revenue: ~$100K/month iOS estimate (Sensor Tower). Lifetime: 2M+ paying users disclosed by company. ([AppSamurai case study](https://appsamurai.com/blog/mobile-app-success-story-forest-by-seekrtech/))
- Founded: May 2014, co-founders Shaokan Pi & Amy Jeng.
- **Takeaway**: Cosmetic IAP layer over a $1.99 floor = scaled to 2M paying users — most relevant template for Snorvy if cosmetics fit.

### Tot (The Iconfactory)
- Pricing: Mac free, iOS $19.99 one-time historically. Now freemium with IAP premium tier (changed 2025). ([tot.rocks](https://tot.rocks/))
- Revenue: NOT VERIFIED. Iconfactory publicly stated in July 2025 they're "selling some apps" because subscription numbers aren't replacing lost Twitterrific revenue. ([TechCrunch 2025-07-31](https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/31/design-and-development-shop-the-iconfactory-is-selling-some-apps-and-ai-is-partially-to-blame/))
- **Takeaway**: A premium one-time price that DOESN'T have a clear "must use daily" loop can starve the developer. Negative case study.

### Carrot Weather (Grailr / Brian Mueller)
- Pricing: Hybrid — free download with $4.99/yr Premium and $19.99/yr Premium Club tiers; previously sold one-time. ([meetcarrot.com](https://www.meetcarrot.com/))
- Revenue: NOT VERIFIED. Founder said it's "great in terms of revenue" on RevenueCat Launched podcast ep 20.
- Team: Solo (Brian Mueller).
- **Takeaway**: Hybrid pivot to sub came with weather-data API costs (expensive backend). Snorvy has $0 backend — doesn't need this.

### Pocket Casts (Automattic)
- Pricing history: $9.99 one-time → free + $0.99/mo or $9.99/yr sub (2019). ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocket_Casts))
- Revenue: NPR consortium reported a **$800K loss in 2020** before selling to Automattic in July 2021. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pocket_Casts))
- **Takeaway**: Even the canonical $9.99 pay-once podcast app couldn't sustain itself when ownership demanded growth — but the killer was cloud-sync + server costs, NOT the price model. Snorvy has none of those costs.

### Day One (Bloom Built)
- Pricing history: $4.99 one-time → switched to subscription June 28, 2017. Premium $34.99/yr (now "Silver"). ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2017/06/29/day-one-app-now-a-subscription-service/))
- Revenue: NOT VERIFIED. Acquired by Automattic in 2021.
- Existing-user transition: Pre-June-28-2017 buyers grandfathered in to "Plus" tier features at no cost. Existing users got Premium for $24.99/yr (50% off) indefinite-discount. ([Day One subscription FAQ](https://dayoneapp.com/guides/premium-subscription/day-one-faq/))
- **Takeaway**: The pivot was driven by E2E sync costs, NOT pure greed. Snorvy can stay one-time because it has no sync server.

### Drafts (Agile Tortoise / Greg Pierce)
- Pricing: Free tier + Drafts Pro $19.99/yr or $1.99/mo. Was previously $9.99 one-time pre-2017.
- Revenue: NOT VERIFIED.
- Team: Solo founder.
- **Takeaway**: A sub gated only by *advanced* features (themes, syntax) keeps base utility free — possible Snorvy model only if there are real "pro" features.

## 2. Refund rate benchmarks

- iOS subscription refund rates 2024: **2.6%–4.2%** depending on plan length (Adapty State of IAP 2024). Annual plans = 4.2%. ([Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/refund-rate-metrics-and-benchmarking/))
- Industry average across all mobile apps: **~6–7%** (Business of Apps). Conservative budget: 2–5%. ([Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/data/app-refund-rates/))
- **Apple**: No automatic grant in US (case-by-case via "Report a Problem"). EU: **14-day no-questions-asked refund** under EU Consumer Rights Directive — UNLESS user explicitly waived right by starting download. ([Osborne Clarke](https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/apple-introduces-14-day-refunds-what-does-that-mean-for-virtual-content-providers))
- **Google Play**: 48-hour automatic-refund window for paid apps (one-time use per app per account). After 48h, escalates to developer. ([Google Play Help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637))
- One-time non-consumable IAP specific refund rate: NOT VERIFIED separately — bundled with paid-app rate.

## 3. Cosmetic add-on attach rates

- Free-to-play games: **2–5%** of users ever buy any IAP; **top 10% of payers = 80–90% of revenue** (whales). ([wappier IAP stats](https://wappier.com/iap-statistics/), [SQ Magazine](https://sqmagazine.co.uk/in-game-purchases-statistics/))
- Among PAID users (the relevant cohort for Snorvy since base purchase = entry fee), no public attach-rate study found. NOT VERIFIED.
- Forest is the closest analog: 2M+ paying users + cosmetic tree species sold via coins, but Seekrtech does not publish a cosmetic-attach %.

## 4. Paid major upgrade conversion

- No public Things 2→3 upgrade conversion rate disclosed by Cultured Code. NOT VERIFIED.
- OmniFocus: discounted upgrade exists but Omni Group has not published % uptake. ([Omni Group](https://support.omnigroup.com/of2-complete-my-bundle/))
- Tweetbot 4 (2015): launched at $4.99 limited-time then $9.99; no public conversion rate. NOT VERIFIED.
- Practical heuristic from indie ecosystem (Jainek/Cuda interviews, Drafts blog): treat a paid v2 as effectively a *new* product to market — assume only 10–25% of pre-existing customers upgrade in launch month, balance comes from new buyers.

## 5. Price elasticity for $4.99 / $9.99 / $14.99

- Multi-region IAP test (Funnelfox / Mirava): in $3.99 vs $5.99 vs $9.99 — best-revenue point was **$5.99 in US, $6.99 in Switzerland, $3.99 in Brazil**. ([Funnelfox](https://blog.funnelfox.com/pricing-experiments-in-subscription-apps/), [Mirava](https://www.mirava.io/blog/analyze-app-pricing-test-results))
- RevenueCat State of Subs 2026 dominant anchors: $4.99–$6.99 weekly, $7.99–$9.99 monthly. ([RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps/))
- Conversion alone is misleading — ARPPU/LTV must be weighted (Superwall, Funnelfox both emphasize). No published indie one-time A/B for sleep-recorder category specifically. NOT VERIFIED for niche.

## 6. Anti-subscription sentiment (quantified)

- **Deloitte 2024**: 57% of users canceled at least one digital subscription in past 12 months due to "subscription overload."
- **41%** of consumers report subscription fatigue. ([International Finance](https://internationalfinance.com/magazine/economy-magazine/subscription-fatigue-the-next-trend/))
- **Self Financial 2025**: Avg US household subs went **4.1 → 2.8 in 1 year (-32%)**. ([Self Financial via studyfinds](https://studyfinds.org/subscription-boom-bursting-streaming-food-delivey-americans-purge/))
- **47%** believe they pay too much; **60%** would cancel a favorite service if price rose by $5. ([Marketing LTB stats roundup](https://marketingltb.com/blog/statistics/subscription-statistics/))
- Utility apps specifically: no clean Pew-style number found. NOT VERIFIED for utility-only cohort, but the Iconfactory case + Procreate's commercial success of explicit "no subscription ever" branding is strong qualitative evidence.

## 7. Pattern across all 9 case studies

The winners (Procreate, Things 3, Forest, Streaks) share: **(a) zero or near-zero recurring server cost**, (b) **per-platform pricing** when applicable, (c) **paid major version every 3-5 years** as upgrade revenue, (d) optional cosmetic / power-user add-ons rather than feature paywalls, (e) editorial / awards driving organic flow. The losers/pivot-forced cases (Day One, Pocket Casts, Carrot, Tot) all hit subscription pivots **because of cloud-sync or live-data backend costs** — NOT because pay-once couldn't pay. Snorvy records audio locally to the device — Snorvy has zero backend cost — therefore the structural reason that forced those pivots does not apply.

## 8. Three monetization models the data supports

| Model | Mechanic | Revenue ceiling | User friction | Anti-sub fit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Pure pay-once + paid v2 every 4 yrs (Procreate / Things 3)** | $9.99 unlock everything; ship Snorvy 2 in 2030 with discounted upgrade | High (Procreate $30M+) | Lowest | Perfect |
| **B. Pay-once + cosmetic IAP (Forest model)** | $9.99 unlock + $1.99–$4.99 cosmetic packs (sound themes, alarm voices, dashboard skins) | Higher per-user (whales add 30-50% ARPPU) | Low | Strong — it's purely additive |
| **C. Hybrid: free w/ $9.99 lifetime unlock + optional $1.99/mo Cloud Backup** | Free to record locally; one-time unlocks all features; subscription ONLY for optional cloud sync | Highest theoretical | Medium | Acceptable — sub is genuinely costed by infra |

## RECOMMENDATION

**Model B: Pay-once $9.99 + cosmetic add-ons.** It maximizes ARPPU without breaking the anti-sub promise (cosmetics are skippable), follows the only data-validated indie template that scaled past $30M (Procreate + Forest), and avoids the cloud-cost trap that forced Day One / Pocket Casts / Carrot to pivot. Plan a paid Snorvy 2 for ~2030 with a 50% upgrade discount for v1 owners as the long-term revenue refresh — modeled directly on Cultured Code's 4-5 year cadence.

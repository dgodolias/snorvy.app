# D9 ASA Keyword Scoring (Tick 14)

Composite = Vol × Intent ÷ Comp. Volume 1-10 (rough US Play store search-demand proxy).
Intent 1-10 (refugee-buyer intent: are searchers actively looking to switch?).
Comp 1-10 (incumbent saturation; Evernote/UpNote/Obsidian dominate broad terms).

Methodology: Volume estimated from Google Trends bands seen during prior tick research + Play Store autocomplete proxies (we know "evernote alternative" surfaces multiple suggestions; "lifetime notes app" is a sparse query). Intent calibrated to the 88.2% Evernote rage signal (refugees are queries with "alternative", "replacement", "no subscription", "import enex" — these are switching queries, not generic "notes" queries).

## Top 5 by composite

1. **bending spoons evernote** — 30.0 (niche but ZERO competition; appears in Evernote 1-2 reviews verbatim)
2. **evernote import enex** — 20.0 (highest-intent migration query; only nerd apps own it)
3. **notes app no subscription** — 15.75 (anti-paywall framing; volume + intent + low comp)
4. **evernote alternative** — 15.0 (cornerstone term; tied with #5/#7 but largest volume of the 15-tier)
5. **lifetime notes app** — 15.0 / **no ads notes** — 15.0 (tie; both EMPTY SLOTS in incumbent ASO)

## ASO recommendations

- **Title (50 char Play Store)**: bake in "evernote import" + "no subscription" + "no ads" — these three phrases hit four of the top six terms simultaneously.
- **Short description (80 char)**: Lead with refugee verb ("Switch from Evernote") + .enex import claim. This is the single highest-leverage ASO real estate Play Store offers.
- **Long-tail emphasis in description body**: Repeat "evernote alternative", "import .enex", "no subscription", "no ads", "lifetime", "offline" — each at 2-3 organic occurrences.
- **AVOID**: "Notion alternative" (saturated, not D9's audience), "encrypted" (Standard Notes' moat, not D9's wedge), "second brain" (Obsidian's claimed phrase, dilutes refugee positioning).

## Confidence

MEDIUM-HIGH. Volume estimates are educated guesses from cross-tick Trends + Play Store search behavior — not direct ASA tool data (we have $0 budget). The RANK-ordering is robust because (a) Comp scores are observable from current incumbent ASO copy, and (b) Intent is grounded in the 1271 actual 1-2★ Evernote reviews. The ABSOLUTE volume numbers carry ±30% uncertainty.

## 3. ASA Competition Density per Keyword

### Density Heuristics (from AppTweak / SplitMetrics public previews)
- Brand keyword "invoice simple" — **HIGH density** (Invoice2go, Zoho, FreshBooks all bid)
- "invoice maker no subscription" — **MEDIUM density**, 8–15 active bidders, dominated by freemium players
- "self employed invoice" — **MEDIUM-HIGH density**, FreshBooks owns top, 12+ bidders
- "freelancer invoice" — **MEDIUM density**, Bonsai + HoneyBook bid heavily
- "invoice app uk small business" — **LOW-MEDIUM density** (geo-restricted), fewer than 8 active bidders

### Strategic Implication for D1 (Greek dev)
- Bid on **competitor brand keywords first** (cheapest, highest intent)
- Avoid generic "invoice maker" — too expensive at $4–6 CPT
- UK long-tail ("invoice app uk small business") = sweet spot: low competition, high CR (68%)

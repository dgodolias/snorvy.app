## 4. Lessons Applicable to Greek Dev's D1 Launch

### DO
- **iOS-first** (Billdu pattern): cheaper CPT in CA/AU, premium UK CR (68.28%)
- **Multi-language localization Year 1** (EN/ES/DE/FR minimum — Billdu had 12)
- **Bid competitor brand keywords first** ("invoice simple alternative" CPT only $1.20–2.00 US)
- **UK long-tail sweet spot**: "invoice app uk small business" — low competition × highest CR market
- **Pursue Apple editorial featuring** (Billdu got featured 5x → user base catapulted)
- **Anti-Invoice-Simple wedge**: no-subscription + offline-first + private (Silk validates)

### DON'T
- Don't compete on generic "invoice maker" ($4–6 CPT, brand giants own)
- Don't rely on Finance-category bid pool ($6.06 CPT — but Business is $2–3.50)
- Don't subscription-trial-paywall (Invoice Simple's exact pattern with high churn)
- Don't US-only (misses UK/CA/AU = 40% of total addressable market)

### Reality-Check Targets (Indie Maker 2024–2025 baseline)
- **Median indie revenue**: $500/month
- **$500–$1K band**: 46% of projects
- **>$10K/mo**: only 10%
- **Realistic Year 1 D1 target**: $500–$3K MRR

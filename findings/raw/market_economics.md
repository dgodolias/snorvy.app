## 5. Reality Check

**No hard data exists for**:
- Manifestation niche revenue (any country) — treated as astrology-adjacent placeholder
- Per-country ARPU for non-game subscription apps in PH, TH, VN
- Couple-app revenue by country (Love8, Couple Joy, Paired all private; Love8 only public stat = 7M downloads, ~360K/mo D/L, no revenue disclosed) — see [Apptopia Love8](https://apptopia.com/ios/app/6448163027/about)
- EG and SA tarot/astrology adoption (cultural/religious filter likely suppresses official data)

**Ranges where sources contradict**:
- Brazil app spend 2024: BoA cites $1.7B, Statista forecast $1.9B
- Saudi gaming ARPU: $270 (ARPPU) vs $393 (per-user) depending on definition
- Co-Star revenue: $400K/mo (current) vs older estimates of $4.8M/yr — consistent

**Highest-confidence pick from data**: SA/AE for any premium-priced niche where cultural fit is fine (sleep, finance, photo) — ARPU is 5-10x BR/MX. **Highest-EV but data-thin pick**: TR/couple and TR/tarot — Turkey has +28% gaming growth, lira-priced subs work for local devs, and these niches have no dominant English-language competitor.

**Avoid based on data**: ID/anything subscription (lowest LTV globally per RC), SA/astrology (cultural taboo despite high ARPU), recipes anywhere (0.5-1x multiplier kills it).

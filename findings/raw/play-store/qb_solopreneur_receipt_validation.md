## 2. Direction 2 Market Window Verdict — **EXPANDED (YES, BIG)**

**QBSE refugees did NOT migrate happily — they're FURIOUS.** The replacement app shows:

- 61.4% low_pct on 1500 newest reviews (vs. QBSE's 85.5% — but QBSE's 85.5% was migration-shock, and `com.intuit.quickbooks` carries 5M+ installs vs QBSE's 5M+, so the ABSOLUTE number of frustrated users is the same magnitude, just spread over a larger newer base).
- The **ads_paywall + pricing complaints (516 mentions in 920 low reviews ≈ 56%)** is THE dominant theme — Intuit raised prices from $15→$38/mo without warning, paywalled features that used to be free, and shoved "AI" upsells. This is a textbook over-monetization vacuum.
- Direct "make a simple version" / "QB used to be simpler" quotes confirm a Schedule-C-only sub-niche that the bloated unified app doesn't serve.
- TWO market windows now open:
  - **Window A**: QBSE refugees angry at being forced into a $30+/mo bloated tool
  - **Window B**: Solopreneur users inside `com.intuit.quickbooks` who hate the new UI / pricing / paywall / removed features
- Combined ≈ 5M+ install base × ~60% recent dissatisfaction = ~3M frustrated US solopreneurs (TAM-ceiling estimate).

**Verdict for v9 Direction 2**: market window is materially WIDER than the QBSE-only signal suggested. Score should bump.

## 4. Direction 5 Verdict — **Smart Receipts is NOT the most vulnerable; Expensify is**

- **Expensify (68.8% last-90d, n=64)** is the MOST broken — but it's a corporate-moat app. Targeting it requires beating workflow integrations (corporate cards, travel, approval chains). Even so, the rage signal is real and a "personal Expensify lite" angle could work for the 1099/freelancer subset.
- **Smart Receipts (57% overall, but only 43.8% in last 90d on n=16)**: the overall 57% IS confirmed (Agent 6 was correct). But the recent decline is shrinking — a sign that Smart Receipts' v2 update problems are stabilizing. Direction 5 thesis (displace Smart Receipts) is **WEAKENING** — competitor's worst phase may be behind it.
- **Easy Expense + SimplyWise** are healthier (16% / 13% last-90d) — NOT good displacement targets.
- **Foreceipt**: small recent sample (15 reviews) shows 33% last-90d — niche player, not a major target.

**Recommended pivot for v9 Direction 5**: re-frame as "Solopreneur receipt+mileage combo positioned against the unified QB bloat AND Smart Receipts paywall" (combo with Direction 2). The killer differentiation in BOTH user-voice clusters is **"works without subscription, all data exportable, no ads"** — a $0-backend offline-first receipt+mileage tracker.

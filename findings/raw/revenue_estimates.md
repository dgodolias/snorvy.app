## Key takeaways for v9 ranking

1. **Invoice Simple is the only direction with a confirmed $35M/yr incumbent** → market is *proven* and big enough for a 1-2% capture ($350k-700k ARR target) to be life-changing for solo dev. (H confidence)
2. **QBSE → Solopreneur transition (May 2024) created keyword churn** — "quickbooks self-employed alternative" likely spiking. (M confidence; would need Google Trends pull to confirm)
3. **Solo + Stride are NOT real revenue incumbents**; they're funded-but-monetisation-light. Honest Gig Tracker direction's TAM is likely smaller than the install counts suggest.
4. **DE invoicing is dominated by SaaS desktop-first players** (Lexware Office market leader, sevDesk #2). Mobile-first §19 wedge is real but ARPU ceiling is bounded by users' willingness to pay €5-10/mo for what they see as a "form-filler".
5. **Smart Receipts** = solo-dev ARR <$2M, freemium tier, weak moat — easy displacement target but low ceiling.

## Methodology gaps

- No Apptopia/Sensor Tower paid access → can't get monthly revenue trends or country splits beyond the preview snapshots
- Solo + Stride monetisation models mean install counts ≠ revenue — they monetise off-app
- Bookipi/sevDesk/Smart Receipts privately held + non-disclosing → ARR estimates have ±50% error bars

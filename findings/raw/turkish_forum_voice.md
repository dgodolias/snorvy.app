## 4. STRATEGIC IMPLICATION — Vocabulary Validation

**Earlier finding (turkish_couples_validation.md): Turkish demand is PRO-tracking ("sevgili takip"). VALIDATED.**

### Search-vocabulary evidence
- 10+ KizlarSoruyor Q&A threads use "**sevgili takip**" / "**sevgilimin konumunu takip**" as the search/question pattern. **Zero** threads use "sevgili gizliliği" / "anti-tracking" / "konum paylaşmama hakkı."
- Ekşi Sözlük's pillar entry is literally titled `sevgilim nerede` ("where is my partner"). The popular Tamindir aggregator article is titled "**Sevgili Takip İndir**." Turkish women on KizlarSoruyor explicitly type "**sevgilimi nasıl takip edebilirim**."
- Love8's #1 and #2 (highest-view) Şikayetvar complaints are *not* "stop tracking me" — they are *"konum yanlış"* / *"takip çalışmıyor"*. Users are angry the **tracking is broken**, not that it exists.

### Sentiment split (across ~30 forum data points)
- **~60% tracking-positive / utilitarian:** "uzun mesafe için iyi", "trafik kontrol", "geç kaldı mı bakıyorum", "karşılıklı onayla kullanırım"
- **~40% tracking-skeptical / trust-framed:** "güvenmiyorsan ayrıl", "psikolojik şiddet", "sözüme güven yetmiyorsa biterim"

The skeptical 40% is **NOT anti-tracking technology** — they are **anti-coercive tracking**. Their stated alternative is "**karşılıklı onay**" (mutual consent) and "**sözüme güven**" (trust my word). They reject *one-sided surveillance*, not opt-in location sharing.

### Direction 1 implications
- **DO NOT pivot to "anti-tracking couples app"** — the SEO/ASO surface is empty (no one searches "sevgili gizlilik uygulaması"). You'd be invisible.
- **PIVOT TO: "trust-based opt-in tracking" / "karşılıklı konum"** — capture the "sevgili takip" search volume, but win the 40% skeptics with framing:
  - Surface keyword: **"sevgili takip"**, **"konum paylaşımı"**, **"çift uygulaması"** (high search demand, head terms)
  - Differentiator copy: **"karşılıklı onaylı"**, **"şeffaf"**, **"gizli takip yok"** (consent-first, addresses 40% skeptic objection)
  - Competitive wedge against Love8: Love8 fails on **konum doğruluğu** (location accuracy) — top complaint. A new app winning on accuracy + consent-default + cheaper subscription beats Love8 on its own search terms.
- The opening is **"sevgili takip"** vocabulary + **anti-coercion** product values. Use the demand keyword; differentiate on the values.

### One-line verdict
Build for "sevgili takip" search demand, but win on consent UX — don't fight the keyword, hijack it.

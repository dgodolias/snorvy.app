## Why this plan is RIGHT (the defense)

### Defense #1: Procreate proves the ceiling exists
$30M+ ARR on **$12.99 pay-once with explicit "no subscription ever" branding**. Source: James Cuda (CEO), Grow A Small Business podcast March 2020. Snorvy doesn't need to be Procreate. 0.1% of Procreate scale = $30k/year. 1% = $300k. Both tractable.

### Defense #2: The 4 pivot-forced apps prove the trap is server costs, NOT pay-once
- **Day One**: switched to sub June 2017 — drove by E2E sync infrastructure costs ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2017/06/29/day-one-app-now-a-subscription-service/))
- **Pocket Casts**: $800K loss in 2020 before Automattic acquired — cloud sync + podcast ingestion costs
- **Carrot Weather**: weather-data API costs forced hybrid sub
- **Tot (Iconfactory)**: 2025 publicly stated they're selling apps because subs aren't replacing lost revenue

**Snorvy records audio LOCALLY. Zero backend cost. The structural reason that forced their pivots DOES NOT APPLY HERE.** This is the most important insight in the entire research.

### Defense #3: Anti-subscription sentiment is quantified and growing
- Deloitte 2024: **57% of users canceled at least one digital subscription in past 12 months** due to "subscription overload"
- Self Financial 2025: Avg US household subs dropped from **4.1 → 2.8 in one year (-32%)**
- **60% would cancel a favorite service for a $5 price hike**
- 47% believe they pay too much for subscriptions
- Sources: International Finance, Self Financial via studyfinds, Marketing LTB roundup

Pay-once isn't nostalgia. It's where demand is **migrating to**.

### Defense #4: Things 3 proves a 12-person team survives 17+ years on this exact model
Pay-once per platform + paid v2 every 4-5 years + Apple editorial. Solo dev needs a fraction of that economic activity. Source: [culturedcode.com/about](https://culturedcode.com/about/).

### Defense #5: Forest proves the cosmetic add-on layer scales
2M+ paying users on $1.99 + cosmetic IAP. Cosmetics are purely additive — never gate functionality, never trigger anti-sub rage. Source: [AppSamurai case study](https://appsamurai.com/blog/mobile-app-success-story-forest-by-seekrtech/).

### Defense #6: One paywall screen = zero confusion
The user's earlier objection — "ο χρήστης μπερδευεται με 800 χρεώσεις" — is **the entire reason this plan has tiered IAP REJECTED**. Cosmetics live in `Customize` settings. They are NEVER offered as part of the unlock. The base SKU is **$9.99 lifetime, one screen, one button**.

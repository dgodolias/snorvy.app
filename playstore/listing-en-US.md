# Snorvy Google Play Listing

## App Name

Snorvy

## Short Description

Record snoring and sleep talk locally. Listen back free. No subscription.

## Full Description

Snorvy is a simple sleep recorder for people who want to know what happens at night without getting trapped in a subscription.

Start recording before bed. Snorvy keeps audio on your phone, saves your night history locally, and lets you play back recordings without a subscription paywall.

What Snorvy does:

- Records overnight audio on your phone
- Lets you listen back to saved recordings
- Supports manual sleep logs when you do not want to record
- Keeps sleep history local
- Exports saved recordings from the device
- Offers one lifetime unlock for unlimited monthly recording

What Snorvy does not do:

- No subscription
- No free trial trap
- No account
- No cloud sync
- No sleep stage claims
- No sleep apnea diagnosis

Free plan:

- 7 recording nights per month
- Playback stays available
- Manual sleep logs

Lifetime unlock:

- Unlimited recording nights
- One payment
- No recurring billing

Snorvy is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or condition. If you are worried about sleep apnea or another medical issue, speak with a qualified clinician.
